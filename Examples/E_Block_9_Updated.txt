                                    # Accessibility Check:
                                    - Player_mastery (5) >= Recipe_required (1): TRUE - Recipe accessible

                                    - Material Economy: 20% chance to save 1 material

                                    # Result:
                                    - Simple Torch crafted automatically and perfectly
                                    - Quality upgraded from "Common" to "Good" (quality bonus triggered)
                                    - Saved 1 unit of "Wood Scrap" (material economy triggered)
