## spiritual_wound_special_action_costs_v1

These are GM-authored `actionCostAudit` fragments for an already admitted exchange
in Chaos Sea or Shining Abode, not complete turn responses. The ordinary action,
actor, outcome, matchup and dice evidence remains required. Assume the validated
current wound generation contributes `spiritual_action_cost_burden` of 1 to the
exact acting owner and named operation; read that contribution and the current
resource projection instead of inventing a burden field, resource owner or wound
identity. See `OtherGuides/Afterlife_Combat_Terminology_Glossary.md` for the full rule.
This example does not enable live C4 publication or other unresolved profiles.

Player recovery succeeds against passive timing, with maximum 6, before 4 and
ordinary recovery art tier 0. Pay 1 first, then cap recovery of 3 at 6:

```json
{"actionCostAudit":{"player":{"operationType":"recover_spiritual_power","baseCost":0,"minCost":0,"artTier":0,"effectiveCost":1,"before":4,"after":6}}}
```

`after=6` is action-only. The client records Spend before Gain, never a net
mutation; Spend-triggered causal reactions run between them. The original capped
Gain request is `after - (before - effectiveCost)`, here 3. A triggered loss of 1
produces 4→3→2→5. A triggered gain of 1 produces 4→3→4→6: Gain still requests 3,
but applies only 2. Never force the final ledger to audit.after or enlarge the
original Gain request after a reaction. At before 6 with the same burden and
success, audit.after is 6 and the fixed request is only 1, even if a reaction
later lowers the balance.

Opposition recovery is opposed by the player's `pressure`, with before 1,
burden 1 and art tier 0. Choose the allowed recovery of 0 from the opposed
0..1 range. Payment still occurs; the action-only difference is negative.
This uses the existing opposed-recovery rule, not a separate opposition outcome:

```json
{"actionCostAudit":{"opposition":{"operationType":"recover_spiritual_power","baseCost":0,"minCost":0,"artTier":0,"effectiveCost":1,"before":1,"after":0}}}
```

The same payment rule applies on both sides. For an ordinary failed player
recovery with before 1 and burden 1, recovery is 0 and audit.after is 0: the
failed attempt still pays. Ordinary player success/partial/failure restores
3/2/0 after payment; opposed recovery restores only 0..1 after payment, subject
to the same maximum. With before 0 and burden 1, insufficient funds
reject the action; future recovery cannot fund payment. Without burden, recovery
keeps its ordinary behavior. Zero-amount mutations follow the client's no-op policy.

A player `force_incarnation` has an applicable wound burden 1 and before 6:

```json
{"actionCostAudit":{"player":{"operationType":"force_incarnation","baseCost":0,"minCost":0,"artTier":0,"effectiveCost":1,"before":6,"after":5}}}
```

The same forced operation by the opposition uses the other side. When present,
`incomingAction.finalOperationType` selects the original effective operation:

```json
{"actionCostAudit":{"opposition":{"operationType":"force_incarnation","baseCost":0,"minCost":0,"artTier":0,"effectiveCost":1,"before":6,"after":5}}}
```

These force audits contain exactly seven fields, with `baseCost=0, minCost=0,
artTier=0`: zero tier is an audit convention, not the actor's art level. The only
cost is the sum of applicable owned wound burdens. No burden means no force audit
and no payment; the earlier example's unburdened forced incoming action therefore
has no opposition audit. Insufficient funds reject; incarnation admission/control
rules do not change. Never copy a force_binding base cost onto force_incarnation.

A newly installed wound may require adding this previously absent side audit only
to an unchanged, unexecuted force action. An absent `actionCostAudit` root may be
created with only qualifying sides. Preserve completed prefix, action, actor,
dice and existing sibling audits. No null/scalar replacement, extra authority or
special-art fields are allowed. The client must prove the exact owner's positive
burden, amount and affordability again, including cold replay; replay does not pay
twice. GM must not write resource state/history, bindings or private receipts.

Worked finite-use expiry: an accepted wound burden applies to two unchanged
future `force_incarnation` actions, and its registered remaining use is consumed
by the first action's real Spend. After the owner drains that action and its
causal work, the next owner reconstruction proves zero burden for the second,
still unexecuted action. Its earlier prescribed positive audit is now stale.
The client requests dependent repair: remove only that force side audit, and
remove the empty audit root if no sibling remains. Keep the root and all sibling
audits when another side still has an audit. Never replace it with a fake zero
audit or pay the expired burden. A removal while positive burden remains is
rejected as a missing required audit. This correction preserves the completed
first action, the second action's operation, actor and dice, all non-force audits
and existing sibling evidence; it adds no author fields. The same checks apply
on cold replay, without charging the first action twice.
