# Mortal Item Materialization v1 — transfer and lineage addendum

Use `mortal_item_existing_transfer_v1` and
`mortal_item_split_merge_lineage_v1` from
`E_CLI_Mortal_Item_Materialization.txt`. Transfer resolves exact `itemId`,
preserves the complete envelope/receipt, removes one source copy, and leaves
one destination copy. Split keeps the selected source ID; the client creates
the derived child and parent lineage. Merge keeps the selected survivor and
retires contributors as `merged`; discard retires as `destroyed`. Never emulate
handoff by removal plus receipt-less recreation, and never write client index
transitions from a GM response.
