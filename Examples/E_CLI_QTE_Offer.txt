# QTE Offer Example

## Canon

- QTE is a cinematic tool for bounded scenes in `Mortal World`.
- The GM may write `output/qte_offer.json` only if `game_state/core/game_settings.json.qteEventsEnabled = true`.
- QTE may be offered only on an ordinary player-driven turn, not on system or transition flows.
- A QTE offer turn must not also resolve ordinary state changes for the same situation.
- If the player declines, the client requests a new ordinary GM turn and the same `qteId` must not be re-offered immediately.
- If the player accepts, the scene resolves locally on the client.
- A valid `qte_offer.json` must contain `qteId`, `title`, `offerText`, `introNarrative`, `startChapterId`, `chapters`, and `terminalOutcomes`.
- `startChapterId` is mandatory and defines the opening chapter; array order in `chapters[]` is not mechanically important.
- Every chapter must contain a non-empty `actions[]`.
- Every chapter must contain `chapterId`, `narrative`, and `actions`.
- Every action must contain `actionId`, `label`, `check`, and `routing`.
- Every `check` must contain `type`, `baseDifficulty`, and `primaryCharacteristic`.
- `baseDifficulty` must stay in the range `1..5`.
- Every `chapterId` and `outcomeId` must be unique.
- Every action routing must contain `success`, `partial`, and `fail`, and each branch must point to exactly one target: `nextChapterId` or `terminalOutcomeId`.
- Every chapter must be reachable from `startChapterId`.
- At least one terminal outcome must be reachable through success-routing.
- Every terminal outcome must contain `outcomeId`, `title`, `finalNarrative`, `gmSummary`, and `responseFragment`.
- `primaryCharacteristic` must use a canonical lowercase stat id such as `dexterity`, `speed`, `wisdom`, or `perception`.
- For `BranchChoice`, `check.config.choiceGrade` must be exactly `success`, `partial`, or `fail`.
- For `MashInput`, `check.config.keys`, `durationMs`, `targetPresses`, and `partialThreshold` are required.
- `MashInput` keys must use canonical QTE key tokens: `q`, `w`, `e`, `a`, `s`, `d`, or `space`.
- `MashInput` duration is 750..10000 ms; target presses are 1..80 and must be physically possible for the duration.
- `MashInput` adjusts the effective required press count by `baseDifficulty` and the relevant `primaryCharacteristic`.
- Escape/cancel resolves as fail in MashInput.
- Browser interactive MashInput parity remains #918; browser surfaces must not claim live MashInput support yet.
- For `PatternMemory`, `check.config.alphabet`, `sequenceLength`, `revealMs`, `inputTimeoutMs`, and `allowedMistakes` are required.
- `PatternMemory` alphabet must use unique canonical QTE key tokens: `q`, `w`, `e`, `a`, `s`, `d`, or `space`.
- `PatternMemory` uses a фаза показа to show the sequence and a фаза ввода where the player repeats it from memory.
- `PatternMemory` sequenceLength is 2..12, revealMs is 500..15000, inputTimeoutMs is 1000..30000 and at least `sequenceLength * 300`, and allowedMistakes is 0..`sequenceLength - 1`.
- `PatternMemory` adjusts effective sequence length, reveal/input time, or mistake tolerance by `baseDifficulty` and the relevant `primaryCharacteristic`.
- Perfect repeat resolves success; tolerated imperfect repeat resolves partial; too many mistakes, timeout, or Escape/cancel resolves fail in PatternMemory.
- Browser interactive PatternMemory parity remains #918; browser surfaces must not claim live PatternMemory support yet.
- For `RhythmPulse`, `check.config.pulseCount`, `beatIntervalMs`, `hitWindowMs`, and `allowedMisses` are required; `patternVariation` is optional.
- `RhythmPulse` pulseCount is 2..16, beatIntervalMs is 300..3000, hitWindowMs is 40..1000, and `hitWindowMs * 2` must be less than `beatIntervalMs`.
- `RhythmPulse` allowedMisses is 0..`pulseCount - 1`, and patternVariation may be `steady`, `accelerating`, or `swing`.
- `RhythmPulse` uses Space in the console and must show visual/textual pulse timing so the scene is not audio-only.
- `RhythmPulse` adjusts effective pulse count, hit window, or miss tolerance by `baseDifficulty` and the relevant `primaryCharacteristic`.
- Browser interactive RhythmPulse parity remains #918; browser surfaces must not claim live RhythmPulse support yet.
- For `PrecisionChoice`, `check.config.choices`, `correctChoiceId`, and `timeoutMs` are required; `timeoutGrade` and `decoyHints` are optional.
- `PrecisionChoice` choices must contain 2..8 unique ids, player-facing labels, and grade values `success`, `partial`, or `fail`.
- `correctChoiceId` must point to the single success choice.
- `timeoutMs` is 1000..30000 ms; `timeoutGrade` may be `fail` or `partial`, never `success`.
- `decoyHints` may reference only existing non-success choices and must contain non-empty hint text.
- Console PrecisionChoice shows stable numbered choices, a visible remaining-time cue, and escaped labels/descriptions/hints.
- `PrecisionChoice` adjusts effective timeout or hint clarity by `baseDifficulty` and the relevant `primaryCharacteristic`.
- Timeout resolves as fail by default and may resolve partial when configured; Escape/cancel resolves fail.
- Browser interactive PrecisionChoice parity remains #918; browser surfaces must not claim live PrecisionChoice support yet.
- For `StealthNoise`, `check.config.durationMs`, `startingNoise`, `dangerThreshold`, `noiseDriftPerSecond`, `recoveryPerInput`, `allowedOverThresholdMs`, and `gradeThresholds` are required.
- `StealthNoise` uses a 0..100 noise meter: `startingNoise` must not exceed `dangerThreshold`, `noiseDriftPerSecond` raises current noise, and `recoveryPerInput` lowers it when the player uses the recovery control.
- `gradeThresholds` must define `successMaxNoise`, `successMaxOverThresholdMs`, `partialMaxNoise`, and `partialMaxOverThresholdMs`; partial thresholds must not be stricter than success thresholds.
- Console StealthNoise shows current noise, danger threshold, remaining time, recovery control, and over-threshold warning as readable text.
- `StealthNoise` adjusts effective drift/recovery/tolerance by `baseDifficulty` and the relevant `primaryCharacteristic`.
- Browser interactive StealthNoise parity remains #918; browser surfaces must not claim live StealthNoise support yet.
- For `LockPinSet`, `check.config.pinCount`, `pinWindows`, `timerMs`, `pickDurability`, `maxMistakes`, `pinDriftPerSecond`, and `gradeThresholds` are required.
- `LockPinSet` pin windows use a 0..100 track; each `pinWindows[]` entry must have ordered `min`/`max` bounds and optional one-based `pin`.
- `pickDurability` is 1..20, `maxMistakes` is 0..`pickDurability`, `timerMs` is 1000..60000, and `pinDriftPerSecond` is 0..100.
- Console LockPinSet shows each pin state, target window, current position, remaining time, mistakes, and pick durability.
- `LockPinSet` adjusts effective windows, timer, drift, or mistake tolerance by `baseDifficulty` and the relevant `primaryCharacteristic`.
- Clean all-pins-open resolves success; slow/noisy open resolves partial; broken pick, timeout, too many mistakes, or Escape/cancel resolves fail.
- Browser interactive LockPinSet parity remains #918; browser surfaces must not claim live LockPinSet support yet.
- `responseFragment` is the final local mechanical outcome for an accepted QTE branch.
- Successful terminal outcomes must include positive `experienceGained`.
- For validation purposes, a successful terminal outcome is any outcome reachable by following `success` branches from `startChapterId`.
- The client will convert a successful QTE `experienceGained` into a real increase of the authoritative XP counter in `game_state/player/experience.json`.
- If the local `experience.json` already contains `level`/`playerLevel`, `experience` or `currentExperience`, and `experienceForNextLevel`, the client will also advance the level locally by the standard progression rule.
- `declineHint` and `cinematicJustification`, if present, are shown in the offer prompt, so keep them short.
- `responseFragment` must not use ordinary `image_prompt`; QTE images go through `sceneImagePrompt`, `chapterImagePrompt`, or `outcomeImagePrompt`.
- For reaction checks, the client handles physical key/RU-EN normalization: prompt labels such as `Q / Й`, `W / Ц`, `E / У`, `A / Ф`, `S / Ы`, `D / В`, and `Space` refer to physical QTE keys.
- GM-authored QTE configs do not encode player keyboard layout; normal text input, dialogue, names, and narrative prose remain ordinary text.

## Invalid Patterns

Do not do this in the same GM turn as `output/qte_offer.json`:

```json
{
  "experienceGained": 80,
  "moneyChange": 25
}
```

Do not put ordinary image output into `responseFragment`:

```json
{
  "responseFragment": {
    "response": "Неверно.",
    "experienceGained": 30,
    "image_prompt": "forbidden here"
  }
}
```
