# E_CLI_Afterlife_Story_Memory.txt

Read when: Current afterlife threat/story/Saref change. Follow only the current dispatch/contract; No current story/state delta does not independently activate this topic.
Prerequisites: Current request, authoritative realm and exact active pending/issued packet; [applicable common-core paragraphs](../OtherGuides/GM_Common_Core.md). Topic prerequisites: [Afterlife_Lifecycle.md](../OtherGuides/Afterlife_Lifecycle.md).
Author here: only existing contract-authorized outputs; this reading guide grants no additional authority.
Never author: do not invent client-owned identities, seals or unissued receipts, or mutate pending/canonical state outside the current contract. Legitimate GM-authored receipts and pending closures remain permitted only through their existing explicitly authorized owner/response surface.
Worked examples: [E_CLI_Afterlife_Story_Memory.txt](E_CLI_Afterlife_Story_Memory.txt); paired rules: [Afterlife_Story_Memory.md](../OtherGuides/Afterlife_Story_Memory.md). Whole original examples/negative labels remain below.

EXAMPLE 26B — Внешняя память посмертия (`afterlife_chronicles_v1`)
==================================================

Scenario:
- The afterlife turn creates durable consequences in a Chaos Sea region and in a Guardian scene.
- The canonical file is `game_state/meta/afterlife_chronicles.json`.
- The accepted response uses `afterlifeChronicleUpdates[]`; the client normalizes it into canonical `chronicles[]`.
- Do not put `afterlifeChronicleUpdates` into `output/narrative_response.json`; that player-facing file may contain only `response` and `timestamp`.
- GM-authored updates write `lastEventsDescription` for the current event. They must not write `eventDescriptions[]`: that field is read-only archive memory maintained by the client from prior `lastEventsDescription` values.
- Use this surface instead of Mortal World memory channels. Do not use `worldEventsLog`, `currentLocationData`, `worldMapUpdates`, Mortal NPC files, Mortal quest files, or Mortal faction files to remember afterlife events.
- Valid `scopeType` values are: `chaos_sea_region`, `shining_abode_district`, `guardian_abode`, `guardian_scene`, `resident_scene`, `faction_zone`, `memory_scene`, `source_of_light`, and `saref_story`.
- Visible chronicle prose must be Russian and in-world. Use `посмертие`, `Море Хаоса`, `Сияющая Обитель`, and `смертный мир`; do not write internal English labels such as `afterlife`, `ChaosSea`, `ShiningAbode`, or `MortalWorld` in `displayName`, `lastEventsDescription`, `persistentConsequences[]`, `openThreads[]`, or visible participant names.

Correct reasoning checklist:
- `chronicleId` is the stable identity key. Reuse it when the same region, district, abode, scene, Source of Light event, or Saref story thread changes again.
- `scopeType + scopeId` says what memory surface is being updated. Keep `scopeId` stable and concrete.
- `lastEventsDescription` is the current event summary visible to future GM reasoning.
- `eventDescriptions[]` is archive history; never include it inside `afterlifeChronicleUpdates[]`.
- `persistentConsequences[]` should describe durable state that can affect future scenes. `openThreads[]` should describe unresolved hooks.
- Use `afterlifeChronicleUpdates[]` for afterlife memory even when the consequence feels similar to Mortal `worldEventsLog`.

EXAMPLE 26C — Afterlife Writer's Room (`afterlife_story_outline_v1`)
==================================================

Scenario:
- The GM wants a private flexible plan for Guardian, resident, Shining faction, memory-scene, spiritual combat, and hidden Saref/Wings pacing.
- The canonical file is `game_state/meta/afterlife_story_outline.json`.
- The accepted response uses `afterlifeStoryOutline`.
- This is private GM planning. It is not player-visible text, not a prophecy, and not an order to force future outcomes.
- If the player breaks the expected route, update `afterlifeStoryOutline` instead of ignoring the player's choice.
- Do not use Mortal `plotOutline` / `game_state/quests/plot_outline.json` for Chaos Sea or Shining Abode arcs.
- Hidden `pendingRevelations[]` can mention Saref / Wings only as private no-spoiler planning; player-facing text still must obey reveal-stage rules.

- `pendingRevelations[]` is private. It may contain spoilers, but those spoilers must not leak into `response`, interface text, ordinary faction UI, or player-visible audits before the relevant reveal contract.
- `nextLikelySceneBeats[]` are likely beats, not rails.
- `playerAgencyNotes` must explicitly tell the future GM what not to force.

EXAMPLE 26D — Активные угрозы посмертия (`afterlife_active_threats_v1`)
==================================================

Scenario:
- The afterlife turn advances persistent threats in the Chaos Sea and Shining Abode.
- The canonical file is `game_state/meta/afterlife_active_threats.json`.
- The accepted response uses `afterlifeThreatsToAdd[]`, `afterlifeThreatsToUpdate[]`, `completeAfterlifeThreatActivities[]`, and `afterlifeThreatsToRemove[]`; the client normalizes them into canonical `threats[]`.
- Use this surface for durable afterlife dangers: soul hunters, Chaos Sea storms, hidden Wings cells, faction conspiracies, cursed resonances, and similar pressure.
- Do not use Mortal `worldMapUpdates.activeThreats`.
- `currentActivity` is live state. Complete/fail/cancel/abandon it through `completeAfterlifeThreatActivities[]`; do not null it directly and do not write terminal `activeState` into the live activity object.
- `impactProfile` is one pressure object with `primaryTargetType`, `primaryTargetId`, `primaryTargetName`, `primaryImpact`, and `baseImpactValue`; it can matter later, but it does not bypass the separate contracts for spiritual combat, Shining factions, relationship gates, chronicles, global flags, or Saref story state.
- `visibleToPlayer=false` means hidden GM context. Do not reveal the hidden threat name, `sarefLink`, hidden cell, or private activity details in ordinary player-facing UI before a reveal.
- `sarefLink` can connect a threat to `Крылья над Бездной`, but it does not reveal Сареф or Крылья Ангелов by itself.

- Use `threatId` as the stable identity key. Do not create a new threat id for the same continuing danger.

- If a threat creates durable public memory, also write `afterlifeChronicleUpdates[]`; if it creates an irreversible fact, also write `afterlifeGlobalFlagUpdates[]`; if it affects Сареф, obey the Saref reveal contract.

EXAMPLE 26E — Политика Хранителей Моря Хаоса (`chaos_sea_guardian_politics_v1`)
==================================================

Scenario:
- The afterlife turn changes Guardian politics in ordinary Chaos Sea.
- The canonical file is `game_state/meta/chaos_sea_guardian_politics.json`.

Correct reasoning checklist:
- `unknown` and `shadow` are No-spoiler stages: do not mention Сареф or Крылья Ангелов in visible player text, and do not store revealed Saref/Wings proof in this state.
- `wings_revealed`, `infiltration_active`, `confrontation_available`, and `completed` require route evidence from Guardian questline fragments and advantages.
- Route evidence is sufficient only if the state has all four mandatory revelation categories, or three mandatory categories plus two additional fragments, or two mandatory categories plus four additional fragments.
- The canonical categories are `identity`, `method`, `faction`, and `path`.
- Each Guardian questline stores `questStates[]` with `questOrdinal = 1..4` and status `latent`, `recognized`, `active`, `ready_to_turn_in`, or `completed`.
- Quest 4 can unlock `sarefRevelation` / `sarefAdvantage` only after quests 1-3 for the same Guardian are completed.
- Quest 4 is not an ordinary Mortal World life and not `Memory Gates`: it closes through the playable layer `Воспоминание`. It does not create `pendingMemoryLegacy` unless the player separately uses Memory Gates. A valid closure uses `sarefMainStoryUpdate.mode=record_memory_scene`, writes `memoryScene` with `layer="Воспоминание"`, `role`, `boundaries[]`, 3-5 `abilities[]`, `requiredStoryNodes[]`, `successCondition`, `closureTarget`, and then persists `guardianQuestlines[].questStates[].memorySceneProof`. Completed quest 4 without that proof is invalid. Evidence is non-physical only: memory, image, echo, knowledge trace, or soul resonance.
- `sarefAdvantages[]` are usable state, not loose story notes. Each advantage has `state=available|spent|passive|disabled|suppressed` and non-empty `applicableScenes[]`; supported scene ids include `wings_infiltration`, `saref_negotiation`, `saref_confrontation`, `oath_break`, `memory_attack`, `faction_conflict`, `escape_or_exile`, `final_resolution`, and `any`.
- If the GM claims an advantage in a scene, record `sarefAdvantageUses[]` with `usageId`, `advantageId`, `sceneType`, `usedAtTurn`, `consumesAdvantage`, and `summary`. The `advantageId` must exist, the state must authorize use, and `sceneType` must match `applicableScenes[]` or `any`.
- A consumed one-use advantage must end as `state=spent`, carry matching `spentAudit` (`usageId`, `usedAtTurn`, `sceneType`, `summary`), and have the matching `sarefAdvantageUses[]` entry. `passive` advantages may be referenced but not consumed. `suppressed`, `disabled`, unknown, or already spent advantages cannot be used for a new scene.
- A mortal objective item is never transferred as a physical mortal item; store only memory, image, echo, knowledge trace, or soul resonance proof.
- Do not use Mortal quest files, Mortal faction files, or ordinary lore notes as authority for this hidden mainline state.
- Keep arrays unique by ids such as `revelationId`, `advantageId`, `outcomeId`, and `endingId`.
- Hidden Wings agents must not all be one archetype. Use a mix of `deceived`, `oathbound`, `fanatic`, and `opportunist`, and give important agents at least one route: `persuade`, `free`, `expose`, `blackmail`, or `defeat`.
- Before reveal, a materialized Shining faction with `sarefFactionRole=wings_of_angels` and `sarefVisibility=hidden|rumored` is hidden from normal faction UI and player-facing JSON audits. After reveal it must become `sarefVisibility=revealed` and `factionLinks.wingsFactionId` must point at that actionable `factions[]` entry.
- The final confrontation with Сареф cannot resolve off-screen. A victory or deal must use `sarefMainStoryUpdate.mode=record_final_confrontation`, write `finalConfrontation.status=resolved`, `directScene=true`, `resolvedAtTurn`, `routeType`, `victoryTier`, `sarefOutcome`, `wingsFactionOutcome`, route proof where needed, and any `advantageUseIds[]`.
- Supported final `routeType` values are `combat`, `political`, `oath_law`, `metaphysical`, `hybrid`, and `deal`; supported `victoryTier` values are `pyrrhic`, `clean`, `deep`, and `deal`. `deep` requires four different Guardians with quest 4 completed. `hybrid` requires `routeComponents[]` with at least two non-hybrid route types and their proof fields.
- A deal uses `routeType=deal`, `victoryTier=deal`, `sarefOutcome=allied`, `wingsFactionOutcome=joined`, root `playerOathState.state=oathbound`, and `endings[].rewardBundle` with `resourceReward`, `wingsAccess`, `sarefArt`, `sarefPassive`, and `oathCost`.
- A victory ending uses `endingType=victory`, references the matching `finalConfrontationId`, and records `rewardBundle.antiOathProtection`, `antiForeignProtection`, `guardianRelationshipEffects[]`, plus a `relic` or `passive`; `deep` also requires both relic and passive plus `deepWorldStateEffects[]`.
- If final victory says the Крылья Ангелов are `broken` or `dissolved`, the linked Shining faction must carry matching `factionLifecycle.state` (`broken` may also accept `dissolved` as stronger closure).
