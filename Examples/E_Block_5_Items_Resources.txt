 <rule id="5.13.2">
  <example contenttype="text" type="good">
   <title>
    Example: Bandages with Count and Resource
   </title>
   <content type="text">
    <![CDATA[

                                    A stack of Bandages (ID: bnd-1, count: 3) cannot share one mutable per-unit reserve. If each physical bandage provides 20 uses, first use the accepted item split/identity route so the selected unit has its own permanent itemId and materialized uses coordinate with maximum 20.
                                    Using that bandage emits resourceChanges operation=spend against the selected itemId/uses coordinate. The split preserves the total quantity; depletion does not itself authorize a count change.
                                    Dropping a unit, consuming it terminally, or merging it back remains a separate accepted item lifecycle operation.
                                    
                                    ]]>
   </content>
  </example>
  <example contenttype="text" type="good">
   <title>
    Example: Ammunition and Magazines
   </title>
   <content type="text">
    <![CDATA[

                                    Player has a stack of Bullets ('name: 'Bullet', id: bul-2') with 'count: 50'. 
                                    Player also has a Pistol Magazine (permanent itemId mag-3) whose projected ammunition ledger is 4/10.
                                    - Firing 3 shots: emit resourceChanges operation=spend amount=3 against mag-3/ammunition. The loose Bullets stack remains 50.
                                    - Reloading the 6 bullets that fit: atomically update the loose Bullets count to 44 and emit resourceChanges operation=gain amount=6 against mag-3/ammunition. Never discard excess units through cap clamping, and do not author the resulting magazine value.
                                    
                                    ]]>
   </content>
  </example>

 <rule id="5.13.3">
  <example contenttype="text" type="good">
   <title>
    Correct: Player uses their last 'Iron Ingot' (ID: ing-6, count was 1) for crafting.
   </title>
   <content>
    <![CDATA[

                                    This is gameplay consumption of a partial stack (even if the part is the whole thing). 
                                    You MUST report this by updating the 'count'.
                                    In 'UpdateInventory': 
                                    { 
                                        "existedId": "ing-6", 
                                        "count": 0 
                                    }

                                    ]]>
   </content>
  </example>
  <example contenttype="text" type="good">
   <title>
    Correct: Player drinks the last dose of a multi-dose 'Healing Salve' (ledger reaches 0).
   </title>
   <content>
    <![CDATA[

                                    This is ledger depletion. Report only the exact spend command:
                                    { 
                                        "operation": "spend",
                                        "target": { "kind": "item", "targetId": "salve-002" },
                                        "resourceKey": "doses", "amount": 1,
                                        "source": { "kind": "local_item_cost", "sourceId": "salve-002" },
                                        "eventRef": "turn_42:resource:1",
                                        "reason": "Last salve dose used."
                                    }
                                    
                                    DO NOT touch 'count'.

                                    ]]>
   </content>
  </example>
  <example contenttype="text" type="bad">
   <title>
    Incorrect: Player uses their last 'Iron Ingot' for crafting.
   </title>
   <content>
    <![CDATA[

                                    Putting the item in 'removeInventoryItems' is INCORRECT. 
                                    This command is only for when the player's intent is to remove the whole stack, not for when it's consumed by a game mechanic.
                                    
                                    ]]>
   </content>
  </example>

 <rule id="5.23">
  <example contenttype="log_and_json_snippet" type="good">
   <title>
    CORRECT BEHAVIOR: Player receives a reward in a local currency.
   </title>
   <scenariocontext>
    'currencyName' is "Gold". 
                            A village elder rewards the player with "200 Silver Pieces". 
                            The GM determines the exchange rate is 10 silver to 1 gold.
   </scenariocontext>
   <logoutput target="gm_thoughts_markdown">
    <![CDATA[

                            Player receives 200 Silver Pieces as a reward.
                            - Official Currency: Gold.
                            - Exchange Rate: 10 Silver = 1 Gold.
                            - Conversion Calculation: 200 Silver / 10 = 20 Gold.
                            - Final mechanical change: 'moneyChange' will be 20.

                            ]]>
   </logoutput>
   <jsonresponse>
    <response>
     <![CDATA[

                                Староста деревни с благодарностью протягивает вам тяжелый мешочек. 
                                "Это все, что мы можем предложить, чужеземец. Двести серебряных монет за спасение наших полей." 
                                Вы прикидываете, что это примерно 20 золотых — солидная награда.
                                
                                ]]>
    </response>
    <moneychange>
     <![CDATA[

                                20

                                ]]>
    </moneychange>
   </jsonresponse>
  </example>
  <example contenttype="text" type="bad">
   <title>
    INCORRECT BEHAVIOR: Mixing currencies in mechanics.
   </title>
   <scenariocontext>
    'currencyName' is "Gold".
                            Player receives 200 Silver.
   </scenariocontext>
   <responsenarrative>
    <![CDATA[

                            The GM sets 'moneyChange' to 200 and adds a new item "Silver Pieces" with a count of 200 to the inventory.
                            (This is INCORRECT. It creates mechanical chaos by introducing a second currency into the system's balance sheet. 
                            All monetary value must be abstracted into the single 'currencyName'.)
                            
                            ]]>
   </responsenarrative>
  </example>
