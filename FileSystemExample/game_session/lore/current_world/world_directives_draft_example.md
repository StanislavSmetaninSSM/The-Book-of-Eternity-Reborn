## World Title
Silver City

## Genre
Gothic Mystery / Urban Fantasy

## Era
Late 19th century

## Tone
Melancholic, elegant, tense

## Setting Summary
A foggy industrial capital where newspapers, salons, occult clubs, secret orders,
and social unrest collide every night.

## Hard Rules
- Public magic immediately attracts attention.
- Firearms exist, but they are rare and regulated.

## Required Elements
- Urban intrigue
- Secret societies
- Class tension

## Forbidden Elements
- Modern internet
- Open dragons and classic high fantasy kingdoms

## Special Mechanics
- Reputation in closed circles matters.
- Public occult incidents change district mood and access.

## Continuity Notes
- The city must react to major scandals and supernatural disturbances.

## Player Amendments
- Prefer mystery, conspiracy, and mood over open warfare.
