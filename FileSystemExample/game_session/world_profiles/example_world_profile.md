# Desert Theocracy of Glass

## Description
A harsh sacred desert realm ruled by temple-cities, ritual law, water monopolies, and visions seen through polished obsidian and glass.

## Genre
Religious intrigue / survival fantasy

## Era
Late bronze to early iron style civilization

## Tone
Severe, sun-bleached, solemn, prophetic

## Hard Rules
- Water is political power.
- Public blasphemy has immediate consequences.
- Miracles exist, but they are interpreted through temple authority.

## Required Elements
- Temple courts
- Pilgrimage roads
- Sacred reservoirs
- Desert storms

## Forbidden Elements
- Gunpowder armies
- Casual modern speech
- Lighthearted parody tone
