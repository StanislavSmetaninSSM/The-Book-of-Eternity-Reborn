This selects joint I publication/result continuity, not complete historical N→I or HOT family availability. ## Crash, compensation and terminal rules

- Before Prepared commit: no I effect has been authorized; checkpoint2 missing result on restart holds, including possible lost computation. Never infer I-unrun or sample again.
- Prepared committed: resume frozen publication only. During second-cohort application, uncommitted recovery restores I-before plus Prepared; committed recovery retains I-after plus Published and settles cleanup. Existing publisher callbacks may retry stored-byte publication after a confirmed rollback; that is not normalization replay.
- Published before common: bypass generic baseline restore/retry; hold or resume current owning admission. Unknown bytes, source drift, replaced G or unresolved journal retain evidence.
- Common rollback/compensation: restore the exact Published pending before-image and retain I-after, not the pre-I baseline. Common commit consumes pending in the marker cohort; late/cold must never repeat common costs/effects. Existing unapplied/applied progression handles its own once-only transition and preserves child3 in immutable phase hashing.
- Terminal abandonment: only the real terminal owner may remove the whole exact pending graph with baseline/readback and confirmed claim release; uncertain removal stays owned/held. No child3 from Prepared, no receipt-only deletion, no permission to restart I for the terminated operation.
- Cold parent2/child1 retains the old supported behavior. Cold child3 validates full current original/history/footprint/progression plus exact I-after and all new links, then returns stored data. It does not run I, resample its UTC, repeat committed ticks/costs/actions, or reconstruct a take. Fresh deterministic planning after an uncommitted rollback is distinct from repeating an already committed effect.
