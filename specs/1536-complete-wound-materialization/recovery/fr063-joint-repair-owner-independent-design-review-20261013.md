The real adapter then validates the corrected complete proposal against the original opportunity. FR-004 permits the applicable severity choice, and FR-010 preserves valid unrelated content. The sealed maximum is a ceiling, not a required severity.

**Required minimum revision:** retain III→II as the first positive fixture/example. Production must accept any correction that passes the actual adapter and the genuine packet's permitted severity range, with the existing materialize/opportunity/woundRef, unaffected proposal, scene, source/generation and final-guarantee checks intact. ## Other reviewed choices and mandatory implementation constraints

- **Owning-contract amendment is explicit and necessary.** The existing six-argument publisher is genuinely homogeneous: `MortalTreatmentPublication.cs:1041–1076` refuses opportunities/transitions, its prepared proof at 732–791 is singleton, and the current common context/plan/cache exclude anchored acquisition plus treatment authority (`AcceptedMechanicsPlan.cs:869–912,1408–1440`; cache 891 onward). The proposed literal amendment retains the old entry and public/raw mixed-command refusal. It serves the existing FR012/FR063 persistence and completion requirement; it is not a new gameplay concept requiring reconfirmation.
- **Admission and cold provenance have identified owners.** The callback precedes the homogeneous treatment branch under the actual original/lease and shared continuity checks. It distinguishes rejection from non-applicability and carries phase-specific private results. Homogeneous physical command plus separately adapter-issued acquisition contribution avoids the occupied command-slot conflict. Cold reading must reconstruct the real rejected adapter result and packet from retained rejected bytes plus the official original, then independently validate the corrected input. A parsed checkpoint, matching hashes, diagnostic claim inspection or a serialized packet alone grants no authority. QTE and treatment event vectors remain separately owner-validated even though the frame agrees.
- **One combined reduction is the correct boundary.** The design explicitly requires one prepared lineage, one effect schedule including existing periodic effects, one root/history construction, deterministic acquisition-then-treatment history order, one common resource reduction and one final guarantee against the whole candidate. It does not merge independently finalized JSON or call two old publishers. Joint anchors and final resource authority need their own sealed family dispatch while old singleton checks remain unchanged. These are substantial SOURCE obligations, not existing API capabilities established by this review.
- **Restore/install and terminal settlement are specified concretely.** Existing rollback backup/hash validation must precede one duplicate-free owned baseline/command/pending batch using the existing trusted publisher, followed by complete readback before repair dispatch. Private command/pending/checkpoint paths stay outside GM writable targets. The proposed terminal token reuses real `PrepareActiveRelease`/`ReleasePreparedUnchecked`, with no monitor held across I/O and post-I/O revalidation/compensation. For a confirmed hold, use its supported terminal reason (`cancelled` or `validation_failed`); the existing `rolled_back` reason is provisional-only. Uncertainty must not fall into ordinary destructive rollback or be reported as a completed release.
- **Completion proof covers the union.** The proposed separately domain-sealed v2 marker, complete treatment history/resource-finalization agreement, fixed pending footprint and exact whole-wave consumption address the limits of the current v1 acquisition marker and request-row-only quarantine. Marker presence alone is not completion. Recovery, all-member readback and typed receipts must precede completed proof. A changed decision after commit must preserve the entire joint union and original continuation opportunity.
- **Cold and new-root responsibilities remain distinct.** The future fresh child must consume an actual live-owned persisted checkpoint, not a fixture-made packet or parent authority. New-root evidence transfers a quiescent, complete durable checkpoint and existing generation, without rewriting originals or hiding unresolved storage decisions. This remains distinct from SaveGame/LoadGame and requires its own actual evidence. Component pending2, ordinary13 and diagnostic API5 cannot substitute for it.
