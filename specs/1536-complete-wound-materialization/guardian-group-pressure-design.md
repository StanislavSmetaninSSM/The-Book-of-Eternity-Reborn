## Approaches and decision

1. **Selected engineering approach:** extend the existing complete
   `ExistingPressureComparisonGroup` and its private `GuardianActualBasis` to all
   authenticated guardian members. Preserve v5 at N=1; emit a closed v7 group role
   at N>=2. Reuse ordinary evaluation, projection, source/resource owners, receipt
   schemas and C3/C4. This changes the missing cardinality/evidence contract without
   introducing another authority path.
2. Widen v5 to a polymorphic singleton/list receipt. Rejected: v5 is a supported
   immutable singleton contract. Historical supported receipts need not be rewritten
   merely because unsupported pre-release saves have no compatibility guarantee.
3. Reuse the player basis or add a guardian evaluator/cache/exact-two lane. Rejected:
   player basis has different failure/actor authority; duplicate owners invite stale
   checks, and an exact-two ceiling is not the accepted distinct-wound rule.

plus one receipt/History parse, checking every creation. Player signed readers and

Share list ordering/global-root checks through `ReadPressureParents`; keep the

## Optimization and freshness contract

Owner remains `SpiritualWoundSourceSession`'s disposable ordinary `ValidationOperation`
under the existing serialized continuation gate. Retain
`OperationComputation("owned-existing-pressure-ordinary", 3)` and the existing
structural identity/dependency service. Group role and complete ordered DATA already
distinguish the new input; do not add another cache or bump unrelated revisions
without a concrete changed-algorithm reason. Cache immutable detached DATA only.

The complete key includes actual capture/source/resource/effect/original-prefix/
baseline/physical-lease identities; session/request/snapshot/turn/realm; role,
revision, projection, side, exact actor/action slot; full raw fingerprint and pointer;
every parent canonical envelope, graph fingerprint and exact effect/component;
cold/source/resource/continuation epochs, all draft image path/fingerprints and
physical witness fingerprints. Dependencies retain ordinal, wound/dependency-cut
versions, AP-before state, all contributions/availability, routing and complete
source/graph metadata, including original profiles/resilience/danger via signed inputs.

One stable operation should perform one current and one signed batch and N parent
resolutions, not N whole-history parses. Repeated ordinary requests reuse one actual
source computation; query, miss/evaluation, hit and fresh-check counts are distinct.
Current owner/physical lease/raw/request/signed-origin/dependency opening and closing
checks, resource/effect admission and pre-take fences stay fresh. Mutation, replacement,
phase/owner/lease/revision/dependency change, failure, cancellation, revocation and
disposal invalidate reuse; no operation/permission crosses cold recapture or retry.
Rollback restores durable generation as required while validation epochs retire.

Use independent per-case mutable state and typed calls, never reflection. Capture
complete immutable actual envelopes once per invocation for detached probes; never
cache fixtures/owners or copy old v5 retry-count assumptions. Measure named signing,
parent batches, ordinary query, actual/C1, cold positive or source-only C3, receipt,
C4, rollback, fresh retry, comparison and cleanup. Serialize scalar counters explicitly.
No speedup or runtime cost is claimed from this design; no timeout increases.
