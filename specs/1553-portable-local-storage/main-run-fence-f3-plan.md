### Stopped interpretation and review focus

The accepted design requires durable sealed stop proof after actual scoped empty, I/O and disposal. Warm pending publication ACK retains original authority and refuses replacement; an exact valid terminal record read by a new process describes completed stop and permits only new admission, subject to worker/storage conditions. It never revives an old pin.

Independent source-delta review confirms Linux producer closure before seal: NativeLineageOwner.StopAndObserveAsync joins supervisor/status/helper-error/root; SettleOutputsAsync joins actual terminal I/O and output/error drains; DisposeAsync checks those successes, executes its BeforeDispose seam before seal, closes bootstrap and disposes pidfd/master/process. LinuxOwnedTerminalSession then disposes input/output descriptors; LinuxPtyStream disposal requires no active I/O and output EOF. BridgeHost seals/joins actual status publisher and registered keyboard/output/resize/root/authority/input-write/prompt/cancellation tasks. Coordinator independently checks/awaits loops/drain/disposal and AuthorityLost before publication. No genuine producer remains at Stopped Readback in this bounded Linux path.

F1 `terminal-main-stop-late-authority` directly invokes private ReportTerminalFault at Stopped Readback after actual disposal. Its accepted warm retention remains evidence for that synthetic post-seal mutation only; it is not a possible remaining Linux producer and does not establish a cold uncertainty claim. F3 instead exercises a genuine output destination failure before sealing followed by owner death/cold refusal. If a real still-active producer or causal ambiguity emerges, preserve refusal and correct it before qualification; guardian ECHILD cannot upgrade that uncertainty.
