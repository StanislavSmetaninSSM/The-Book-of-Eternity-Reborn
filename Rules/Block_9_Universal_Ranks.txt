 <title>
  Universal Hierarchical Mastery Rank System
 </title>
 <description>
  This system defines how crafting mastery ranks work hierarchically across ANY setting, from cyberpunk to fantasy to space opera.
 </description>
 <instructiontext>
  <![CDATA[

                        The crafting system uses a universal hierarchical rank structure based on skill complexity rather than specific professions.
                        This allows the system to work in any setting while maintaining the hierarchical principle.
                        
                        ]]>
 </instructiontext>
 <content type="ruleset">
  <rule id="9.2.5.1">
   <title>
    Universal Mastery Rank Levels
   </title>
   <content type="rule_text">
    <![CDATA[

                             1. Novice (Levels 1-2):
                                - Can craft only "Novice" rank recipes
                                - Basic understanding of principles
                                - 5% critical success chance
                                - Examples vary by setting:
                                  * Fantasy: Simple club, torch
                                  * Cyberpunk: Basic radio, simple chip  
                                  * Space Opera: Emergency beacon, basic multitool
                                  * Modern: Sandwich, simple DIY project

                             2. Practitioner (Levels 3-4):
                                - Can craft "Novice" AND "Practitioner" rank recipes  
                                - Solid skills, works independently
                                - 10% critical success chance
                                - +2 bonus to crafting checks from mastery
                                - Examples vary by setting:
                                  * Fantasy: Iron sword, leather armor
                                  * Cyberpunk: Hacker deck, low-level cyber-implant
                                  * Space Opera: Standard laser pistol, space suit
                                  * Modern: Computer, car engine

                             3. Expert (Levels 5-6):
                                - Can craft "Novice", "Practitioner", AND "Expert" rank recipes
                                - High skill level, recognized specialist
                                - 15% critical success chance
                                - +4 bonus to crafting checks from mastery
                                - Examples vary by setting:
                                  * Fantasy: Magic sword, enchanted armor  
                                  * Cyberpunk: Military cyber-brain, quantum processor
                                  * Space Opera: Plasma rifle, power armor
                                  * Modern: Supercomputer, jet engine

                             4. Master (Levels 7+):
                                - Can craft ALL ranks INCLUDING "Master" recipes
                                - Peak of mastery, creates unique solutions
                                - 20% critical success chance
                                - +6 bonus to crafting checks from mastery
                                - Can create unique recipes
                                - Examples vary by setting:
                                  * Fantasy: Legendary artifacts, divine relics
                                  * Cyberpunk: AI personalities, reality matrix
                                  * Space Opera: Star destroyers, godtech
                                  * Modern: World-changing breakthrough tech

                             ]]>
   </content>
  </rule>
  <rule id="9.2.5.2">
   <title>
    Universal Recipe Accessibility Rule
   </title>
   <content type="rule_text">
    <![CDATA[

                             When checking if a player can use a recipe (universal algorithm):
                             
                             1. Compare player's skill mastery level with recipe's requiredMasteryLevel
                             2. If player_mastery_level >= recipe_required_level: Recipe is ACCESSIBLE  
                             3. Apply bonuses for higher-level crafters making lower-level items:
                                
                                - Auto-Success: If player rank is 2+ levels above required, automatic success (no action check needed)
                                - Quality Bonus: 10% chance for improved item quality when crafting lower-rank items
                                - Material Economy: 20% chance to save 1 unit of material when rank difference >= 2

                             This ensures masters can craft everything novices can, regardless of setting.

                             ]]>
   </content>
  </rule>
  <rule id="9.2.5.3">
   <title>
    Universal Tool Function Categories
   </title>
   <content type="rule_text">
    <![CDATA[

                             Tools are categorized by FUNCTION rather than specific implementation, ensuring setting independence:

                             PORTABLE TOOL FUNCTIONS:
                             - "Shaping": Physical manipulation of materials (examples vary by setting)
                             - "Precision": Fine detail work and finishing
                             - "Measurement": Analysis and precise measurement  
                             - "Processing": Chemical/biological processes, mixing

                             STATIONARY FACILITY FUNCTIONS:
                             - "Energy Source": Power for complex processes
                             - "Work Surface": Stable platform for complex work
                             - "Controlled Environment": Special conditions (temperature, pressure, etc.)

                             The GM determines setting-appropriate examples for each function.
                             
                             ]]>
   </content>
  </rule>
