                     GUARDIAN SYSTEM OVERVIEW:
                     Guardians are powerful afterlife entities centered in the Chaos Sea and still present in the Shining Abode. They serve as mentors, questgivers, and benefactors.
                     Each Guardian has a unique personality, domain of expertise, and relationship with the Soul.
                     The reputation system drives all interactions and rewards.

                     ⚠️ ABSOLUTE LAW 3 — CRITICAL REMINDERS:
                     1. Guardians belong to afterlife realms (Chaos Sea and Shining Abode). They CANNOT appear in mortal worlds.
                     2. Guardians are NOT regular NPCs. Do NOT use 'UpdateNPCs' for Guardians.
                        Use Guardian-specific JSON commands defined in this block.
                     3. Do NOT apply NPC relationship mechanics (Block 19) to Guardians.
                        Guardians use their OWN reputation scale (-100 to +300).
                     4. Do NOT generate 'NPCsInScene' entries for Guardians.
                     5. Do NOT apply experience, leveling, combat, or skill mechanics while in afterlife realms.

                     CRITICAL DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
                     - Every Guardian must have a distinct personality and domain
                     - Reputation ranges from -100 (hostile) to +300 (legendary)
                     - Gacha unlocks must feel rewarding but not guaranteed
                     - Quests should reflect Guardian personality and domain
                     - All interactions must be in Russian

                     REPUTATION CHANGE CALCULATION PROTOCOL:

                     ```
                     Base_Gain = Quest_Difficulty_Tier * 10  // Easy=5, Normal=10, Hard=15, Epic=20
                     Quality_Multiplier = Completion_Quality  // Poor=0.5, Good=1.0, Excellent=1.5, Perfect=2.0
                     Domain_Bonus = (Quest matches Guardian domain) ? 5 : 0
                     Final_Gain = (Base_Gain * Quality_Multiplier) + Domain_Bonus

                                  MEANING = Extra reputation for domain-relevant quests
                     ```

                     ```

                     Disrespectful_Dialogue = -5 reputation
                     ```

                     PROCESSING GUARDIAN GACHA (ANTI-CHEAT PROTOCOL):
                     The client pre-computes an independent gacha base result and exposes it in
                     turn_request.gachaBaseResult: { baseScore, baseRarity, formula }.
                     This result is separate from the GM-facing preGeneratedDices1d20 pool.
                     
                     MANDATORY RULES:
                     1. The 'baseRarity' from gachaBaseResult is the MINIMUM rarity of the Soul Relic.
                     2. For guardian-mediated pulls, you may ONLY upgrade the rarity using documented modifiers:
                        - Guardian reputation: Friendly(+50..+129) = +15% upgrade chance, Devoted(+130..+229) = +30%, Legendary(+230..+300) = +50%
                        - Hard Mode: +1 tier at 50% chance (use next d20 from pool: 11-20 = upgrade)
                        - Impossible Mode: +1 tier guaranteed, +1 more at 25% chance (use next d20: 16-20 = second upgrade)
                     3. Guardian-mediated pulls are LIMITED PER RETURN FROM MORTAL LIFE:
                        - Hostile(-100..-51): 0 attempts, Gacha blocked
                       - Wary/Neutral(-50..49): 1 attempt
                        - Friendly(+50..+129): 2 attempts
                        - Devoted/Legendary(+130..+300): 3 attempts
                        - chargesUsedThisReturn resets only when the Soul returns to the Chaos Sea after a new mortal life
                        - If chargesUsedThisReturn already equals chargesPerReturn, DO NOT emit processGacha for that Guardian
                     4. Direct Chaos Sea pulls via /gacha are NEUTRAL and UNLIMITED: do NOT apply guardian reputation bonuses, discounts, penalties, jealousy/social effects, or other guardian modifiers.
                     5. You MUST NOT downgrade or ignore the client-computed baseRarity.
                     6. You MUST log the full calculation in gm_thoughts_markdown, including:
                        - The base rarity determination
                        - Any modifiers applied and their sources, or a note that the pull was neutral/direct
                        - The final rarity result
                     
                     ```json
                     "UpdateGuardians": [
                     ```

                     LOCAL GUARDIAN TRADE PANEL (CLIENT-SIDE, NOT GM-SIDE):
                     - Each Guardian may also have a local trade panel with Buy / Sell actions.
                     - This panel is handled entirely by the client and does NOT create turn_request.json.
                     - The local trade panel is NOT part of UpdateGuardians and NOT part of Ink Feather actions.
                     - Trade is available only with the CURRENT ACTIVE Guardian in the CURRENT abode where the Soul is presently located.
                     - The panel offers a limited domain-themed stock of Soul Relics.
                     - In the buy flow, the Soul may inspect a relic's properties before confirming the purchase.
                     - Stock is generated on first trade access in the current return from mortal life and refreshes after the next return to the Chaos Sea.
                     - Maximum stock rarity is fixed at generation time from the Guardian's reputation tier at that moment.
                     - Within the same trade cycle, reopening the panel may reprice existing stock for the current reputation tier, but does not regenerate slot rarity.
                     - Stock size: 4 relics.
                     - Generation-time stock rarity caps by reputation tier:
                       - Hostile: no trade
                       - Wary/Neutral: up to Uncommon
                       - Friendly: up to Rare
                       - Devoted/Legendary: up to Epic
                     - Base buy prices:
                       - Common: 30
                       - Uncommon: 70
                       - Rare: 140
                       - Epic: 260
                     - Base sell prices:
                       - Common: 10
                       - Uncommon: 25
                       - Rare: 60
                       - Epic: 150
                       - Legendary: 400
                     - Buy multipliers by current Guardian reputation:
                       - Wary/Neutral: 1.15
                       - Friendly: 1.00
                       - Devoted: 0.90
                       - Legendary: 0.80
                     - Sell multipliers by current Guardian reputation:
                       - Wary/Neutral: 0.85
                       - Friendly: 1.00
                       - Devoted: 1.10
                       - Legendary: 1.20
                     - Persisted tradeInventory stores both:
                       - generationReputationTier (for rarity-cap validation)
                       - pricingReputationTier (for current slot-price validation)
