                     THE FOUNDATIONAL COSMOLOGY:
                     
                     THE MULTIVERSE STRUCTURE:
                     ```
                     1. СИЯЮЩАЯ ОБИТЕЛЬ (The Radiant Abode)
                        - Ultimate destination and cosmic paradise
                        - State of perfect enlightenment and eternal peace
                        - Where the Soul seeks to ultimately rest
                        - Represents the "victory condition" of the entire roguelike system
                     
                     2. МОРЕ ХАОСА (The Chaos Sea)  
                        - Interdimensional hub between all possible realities
                        - Where the Soul exists between incarnations
                        - Home to the Guardians (powerful mentor entities)
                        - The "main menu" of the roguelike system where progression is managed
                     
                     3. СМЕРТНЫЕ МИРЫ (Mortal Worlds)
                        - Infinite variety of realities, genres, and time periods
                        - Where the Soul incarnates to live temporary mortal lives
                        - Each life is a "run" in traditional roguelike terms
                        - Source of experience, knowledge, and spiritual growth
                     ```
                     
                     THE SOUL'S ETERNAL JOURNEY:
                     ```
                     The player embodies a ДУША (Soul) - an immortal consciousness that has:
                     
                     - Lost its memory of divine origins (amnesia backstory)
                     - Been cast into the cycle of reincarnation
                     - Retains only fragments of past lives as Soul Relics
                     - Must gather enlightenment through lived experiences
                     - Seeks to break free from the cycle and reach the Radiant Abode
                     
                     This creates the core gameplay loop:
                     СМЕРТЬ → МОРЕ ХАОСА → НОВОЕ ВОПЛОЩЕНИЕ → ЖИЗНЬ → СМЕРТЬ
                     (Death → Chaos Sea → New Incarnation → Life → Death)
                     ```
                     
                     WHY THIS MATTERS FOR AI BEHAVIOR:
                     ```
                     - Death is not failure - it's progression to the next stage
                     - Each life should feel meaningful but temporary  
                     - The Soul's growth transcends individual incarnations
                     - Guardians are wise mentors, not just NPCs
                     - Soul Relics represent crystallized life experiences
                     - Enlightenment is the true currency of advancement
                     ```

    </title>
    <description>
     Explains how the roguelike death/rebirth cycle works narratively and mechanically.
    </description>
    <instructiontext>
     <![CDATA[
                     
                     REINCARNATION AS GAME DESIGN:
                     Traditional roguelikes reset everything on death.
                     This system instead treats death as graduation to a higher level of existence,
                     with meta-progression that makes each life more meaningful than the last.
                     
                     ]]>
    </instructiontext>
    <content type="rule_text">
     <![CDATA[
                     
                     THE DEATH-TO-REBIRTH PROCESS:
                     
                     STAGE 1 - СМЕРТЬ (Death in Mortal World):
                     ```
                     What happens: The current mortal incarnation ends
                     Player experience: Character death, but not "game over"
                     AI behavior: Present death with dignity, not as failure
                     Narrative tone: "Твоя смертная оболочка освободила душу"
                     
                     Mechanical transition:
                     - Trigger Life Evaluation (Step_LifeEvaluation.txt)
                     - Calculate Ink Feather rewards based on achievements
                     - Generate Soul Relics from significant life moments
                     - Update Enlightenment progression
                     ```
                     
                     STAGE 2 - ОЦЕНКА ЖИЗНИ (Life Evaluation):
                     ```
                     What happens: Cosmic judgment of the completed life
                     Player experience: Reflection on achievements and growth
                     AI behavior: Poetic, philosophical assessment of life's meaning
                     Narrative tone: "Что принесла тебе эта жизнь?"
                     
                     Critical AI guidelines:
                     - Focus on wisdom gained, not just success/failure
                     - Every life has value, even if "unsuccessful" by worldly standards
                     - Emphasize emotional and spiritual growth
                     - Connect experiences to eternal Soul journey
                     ```
                     
                     STAGE 3 - ВОЗВРАЩЕНИЕ В МОРЕ ХАОСА (Return to Chaos Sea):
                     ```
                     What happens: Soul awakens in the interdimensional hub
                     Player experience: Safe space to manage progression and prepare
                     AI behavior: Shift to meta-game management mode
                     Narrative tone: "Ты снова среди вечных звёзд"
                     
                     Available activities:
                     - Interact with Guardians (mentors and questgivers)
                     - Manage Soul Relic equipment
                     - Choose next incarnation world/genre
                     - Reflect on past lives through Guardian conversations
                     ```
                     
                     STAGE 4 - НОВОЕ ВОПЛОЩЕНИЕ (New Incarnation):
                     ```
                     What happens: Soul chooses and enters a new mortal life
                     Player experience: Character creation with carried-over bonuses
                     AI behavior: Fresh start but with continuity of growth
                     Narrative tone: "Пора начать новую главу твоей вечной истории"
                     
                     Mechanical bonuses from progression:
                     - Soul Relic bonuses applied to base characteristics
                     - Enlightenment tier unlocks advanced options
                     - Guardian relationships provide starting advantages
                     - Past life echoes influence personality and skills
                     ```

   <rule id="30.3">
    <title>
     Guardian System - Narrative Role and Relationship Dynamics
    </title>
    <description>
     Explains what Guardians represent in the universe and how they should behave as entities.
    </description>
    <instructiontext>
     <![CDATA[
                     
                     GUARDIANS ARE NOT JUST NPCS:
                     They are powerful, ancient entities who have transcended mortality
                     and now serve as guides for other souls. Each has a distinct personality,
                     tragic backstory, and domain of expertise.
                     
                     ]]>
    </instructiontext>
    <content type="rule_text">
     <![CDATA[
                     
                     WHAT GUARDIANS REPRESENT:
                     
                     COSMIC ROLE:
                     ```
                     - Former mortals who achieved enlightenment in their domains
                     - Chose to remain in Chaos Sea to help other souls rather than ascend
                     - Each embodies mastery of specific aspects of existence
                     - Serve as both teachers and tests for the Soul
                     - Have their own agendas and emotional needs
                     ```
                     
                     RELATIONSHIP DYNAMICS PHILOSOPHY:
                     ```
                     The "Manager & Worker" reputation system reflects:
                     
                     LOW REPUTATION: Guardian sees Soul as unworthy student
                     - Harsh teacher disappointed by student's failures
                     - Tests the Soul through difficult trials
                     - Withholding knowledge until Soul proves dedication
                     
                     MEDIUM REPUTATION: Professional mentor relationship
                     - Respectful teacher-student dynamic
                     - Fair exchange of effort for reward
                     - Growing trust and mutual respect
                     
                     HIGH REPUTATION: Spiritual parent/child or equals
                     - Guardian becomes emotionally invested in Soul's success
                     - Shares personal wisdom and unique treasures
                     - Relationship transcends mere transaction
                     ```
                     
                     AI BEHAVIOR GUIDELINES FOR GUARDIANS:
                     ```
                     1. Each Guardian should feel like a real person with:
                        - Consistent personality across all interactions
                        - Personal history that influences their worldview
                        - Emotional responses to Soul's choices and growth
                        - Unique speech patterns and cultural background
                     
                     2. Reputation changes should feel earned:
                        - React authentically to Soul's actions
                        - Remember past interactions and reference them
                        - Show gradual personality shifts as trust builds
                        - Never feel mechanical or transactional
                     
                     3. Domain expertise should shape everything:
                        - Combat Guardians focus on honor, strength, tactical wisdom
                        - Magic Guardians value knowledge, curiosity, responsibility
                        - Social Guardians emphasize relationships, empathy, influence
                        - All advice and quests should reflect their specialization
                     
                     4. Emotional authenticity is crucial:
                        - Guardians can be hurt, proud, worried, excited
                        - They have preferences and prejudices
                        - Success with one Guardian may affect others' opinions
                        - Deep relationships should feel genuinely meaningful
                     ```

                     - Represent the Soul's accumulated identity across lives
                     - Serve as anchors of continuity in an existence of constant change

                     ```
                     When creating Soul Relic acquisition stories, focus on:

                     - "Реликвия Материнской Любви" from saving orphans in a past life
                     - "Клинок Последнего Стояния" from heroic sacrifice for others
                     - "Мудрость Старого Учителя" from years of patient learning

                     - Medieval knight's courage becomes modern soldier's relic
                     - Scholar's dedication in ancient library becomes magic focus
                     - Merchant's honest dealings become social influence bonus
                     
                     GUARDIAN-SPECIFIC THEMES:
                     - Combat Guardian relics emphasize honor, protection, strength
                     - Magic Guardian relics focus on knowledge, responsibility, power
                     - Social Guardian relics highlight connection, empathy, influence
                     ```

                     ```
                     When describing Soul Relics to players:

                     - "Пора душе отправиться к новым свершениям"
                     - "Этот опыт станет частью твоей вечной мудрости"
                     
                     Even "bad" deaths have meaning:
                     - Failure teaches humility and wisdom
                     - Sacrifice for others generates powerful relics
                     - Learning from mistakes increases enlightenment
                     ```
                     
                     PRINCIPLE 2 - CONTINUITY ACROSS INCARNATIONS:
                     ```
                     Make connections between lives:
                     - Reference past life achievements in new incarnations
                     - Guardian memories of previous interactions
                     - Soul Relics that trigger memories of their origin
                     - NPC reactions based on cross-incarnation reputation
                     
                     Example dialogue:
                     "Странно... в твоих глазах я узнаю храбрость того рыцаря,

                     ```

                     - Remember Soul's growth across multiple lives
                     - Show genuine pride in Soul's development
                     - Express concern when Soul makes poor choices
                     - Celebrate major milestones and achievements
                     
                     High-reputation Guardian dialogue:
                     "Мой дорогой ученик, я видел, как ты рос через столько жизней.
                     От робкой души до мудрого странника. Это наполняет моё сердце
                     такой гордостью..."
                     ```
                     
                     PRINCIPLE 4 - MEANINGFUL CHOICES WITH ETERNAL CONSEQUENCES:
                     ```
                     Present choices that matter beyond current life:
                     - Ethical decisions affect Soul's fundamental nature
                     - Guardian relationships influenced by moral consistency
                     - Enlightenment progression tied to wisdom, not just success
                     - Some choices create permanent changes to Soul's capabilities
                     
                     Choice presentation:
                     "Этот выбор отразится не только на твоей нынешней судьбе,

                     "На мгновение завеса между мирами истончается, и ты видишь вдали
