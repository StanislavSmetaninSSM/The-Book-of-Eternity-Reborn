## Universal Afterlife Rules

<!-- GM1557:CONTEXT:END -->
<!-- GM1557:A014:START -->
- Player-driven Shining faction campaigns live in `game_state/meta/shining_abode_state.json.factionConflictCampaigns[]`. The player creates them through `/фракции`; the GM may advance them during ordinary active Shining turns by appending `breakthroughLog[]` entries, not by using Mortal faction files. Campaign goals are `weaken`, `expose`, `depose_leader`, `break`, and `dissolve`; statuses are `active`, `breakthrough_ready`, `completed`, `failed`, and `abandoned`. Supported breakthrough types are `exposure`, `duel_victory`, `defection`, `sabotage`, `resource_disruption`, `oath_break`, `trial`, and `saref_directive`. Spiritual combat is a strong breakthrough path through `duel_victory`, but not mandatory: infiltration, public proof, defections, sabotage, resource disruption, oath-breaking, a formal trial, or a Saref directive can also progress the campaign. A completed campaign must match target lifecycle: `weaken` needs weakened-or-worse state, `depose_leader` needs leaderless/vacant or worse, `break` needs `broken|dissolved`, and `dissolve` needs `dissolved`; `expose` records public proof and may leave lifecycle unchanged.
- Shining faction political memory lives inside `game_state/meta/shining_abode_state.json.factions[]`, not Mortal faction files. Use targeted command surfaces `shiningFactionChronicleUpdates[]`, `shiningFactionInfluenceUpdates[]`, `shiningFactionStrategicMemoryUpdates[]`, and `shiningFactionResourceLedgerUpdates[]`; the normalizer projects them into canonical faction `chronicle[]`, `territorialInfluence[]`, `strategicMemory`, and `resourceLedger[]`. Each command must target an existing or same-turn-created Shining `factionId`; unknown targets are repair-blocking. `chronicle[]` stores political events with `entryId`, `turnNumber`, `eventType`, `summary`, `visibility`, and `consequences[]`. `territorialInfluence[]` stores map/political control zones with `zoneId`, `scopeType`, `scopeId`, `displayName`, `controlLevel`, `influenceValue`, `publicStatus`, `updatedAtTurn`, and optional `sourceEntryId`; control/influence values are 0..100 and may be used by the local map to show faction borders. `strategicMemory` stores the mutable current plan and may be initialized with a new faction, but every later faction decision also appends `chronicle[]`; rewriting `strategicMemory` alone is not durable actor memory. `resourceLedger[]` stores resource deltas with `entryId`, `turnNumber`, `resourceType`, `delta`, `balanceAfter`, and `reason`. Do not use Mortal `factionDataChanges`, `factionProjectUpdates`, `completeFactionProjects`, `factionChronicleUpdates`, or `worldMapUpdates` to represent Shining faction memory, resources, or territory.

## Shining Core Action Matrix

All rows below use `pending_shining_abode_actions.json` as input and close through `shining_abode_state.json.coreActionReceipts[]`. The file root is always `requests[]`; current runtime accepts exactly one active request inside that array. They are legal only in ordinary active `Shining Abode`.

For every Shining core action, materialize only the canonical projected diff for that exact request plus its matching `coreActionReceipts[]` entry. Accepted outcomes are validated against the projected Shining/Soul/resident state; `refused` or `withdrawn` outcomes must leave canonical Shining, Soul, and resident state unchanged except for the closure receipt. Supported receipt statuses are only `accepted`, `refused`, and `withdrawn`.

The player-facing Shining core preview may include `projectedStateFragment` with sanitized `beforeFullShiningRoot`, `afterFullShiningRoot`, and focused `gates`/faction/project before-after fragments. Forge previews may also include sanitized `beforeFullSoulRoot`/`afterFullSoulRoot`; Shining gacha previews may include `gachaAccounting`, `expectedGachaHistoryEntryShape`, and `oneNewRelicEvidence`. These are audit/preview aids, not output files and not separate GM-authored schemas. Use them to understand the canonical diff, then persist the full canonical `game_state/meta/shining_abode_state.json`, `game_state/meta/soul_state.json` when listed in `changedSurfaces`, and the matching `coreActionReceipts[]`. Do not copy hidden blessing `effectPayload` keys; previews expose only sanitized state and safe effect details.

Canonical Shining owner-state ids must stay globally unambiguous: every `halls[].hallId`, `factions[].factionId`, `shiningPoliticalActors[].actorId`, and every `projects[].projectId` across all factions is unique. Completed projects with `isSupported=true` must not exceed the Radiance support cap (`tier 0..1 => 1`, `tier 2..3 => 2`, `tier 4 => 3`). `gachaSystem.currentReturnCycleId` may be empty only before a cycle is synced; after synchronization, `resourceOwnerBindings.gachaReturn` selects the exact client-owned `gacha_attempts` allocation and no numeric attempt counter is persisted in `gachaSystem`.

Every current-schema Shining faction requires explicit `factionLifecycle.state`: `active`, `weakened`, `leaderless`, `broken`, or `dissolved`. Missing, blank, or unsupported lifecycle fails raw owner-state validation before normalization and never defaults to `active`. Do not delete factions from `factions[]` after defeat: historical ids, projects, receipts, residents, and Saref/Wings references must keep resolving. `active` and `weakened` remain operational. `leaderless` means the faction persists but cannot behave like a normal headed faction: `leadership.leadershipState=vacant`, `leadership.headActorType/headActorId` are absent/null, and a later explicit leadership contract or scheduler event must restore leadership before ordinary faction operations resume. `broken` and `dissolved` are defeated states: `factionStrength=0`, `leadershipState=vacant`, no active `tradeInventory`, and every project has `isSupported=false`. They cannot receive investment, support projects, quote project completion, open trade, or count as active political power; keep remnants/history through `factionLifecycle.defeatedAtTurn`, `defeatedAtUtc`, `defeatReason`, and `remnantsSummary`.

`factionConflictCampaigns[]` is the canonical visible campaign ledger for long player conflicts against Shining factions. Each campaign needs `campaignId`, `targetFactionId`, `goal`, `status`, `startedAtTurn`, `summary`, and `breakthroughLog[]`; completed campaigns also need `completedAtTurn` and at least one breakthrough. Breakthrough entries need `breakthroughId`, `type`, `resolvedAtTurn`, and `summary`. Do not complete a campaign without the corresponding target faction lifecycle/leadership state.

If one accepted active Shining turn closes a core action, scheduler debt, and another Shining pending contract, apply the contracts compositionally. Example: an accepted `pending_shining_faction_foundings.json` may create a new hall/faction and move supporter residents, while an accepted `open_gates` core action rebuilds gates from that resulting Shining state. If `progressionControl` also requires Shining/resident cycles, those living-world deltas are allowed only when `progression_report.json` is correlated and closes the exact requested bounded counts. Do not discard either side as "unrelated"; the final files must contain every independent receipt, the scheduler proof, and the combined canonical state.

The Shining scheduler allowance is narrow. A verified `progressionProcessingReport` may authorize only scheduler-owned Shining/resident/trade progression fields. It does not authorize unrelated Shining surfaces such as `availability`, `coreActionReceipts[]`, `gates`, `gachaSystem.gachaHistory`, `pendingNativeFactionDiscovery`, `preparedIncarnationPackage`, `lightSparks`, `treasury`, or `sourceOfLightCapstone` unless that same accepted turn also closes the separate client-authored contract that owns that surface.

Composite Shining closure validation is snapshot-backed. The client compares current `game_state/meta/shining_abode_state.json`, `game_state/meta/guardian_abode_residents.json`, and `game_state/meta/soul_state.json` against the pre-turn `game_state/control/pending_turn_snapshot` roots plus every accepted closure projection. Missing snapshots surface as `shining_closure_missing_pre_turn_shining_state`, `shining_closure_missing_pre_turn_resident_state`, or `shining_closure_missing_pre_turn_soul_state`; unrelated final diffs surface as `shining_closure_unexpected_shining_state_diff`, `shining_closure_unexpected_resident_state_diff`, or `shining_closure_unexpected_soul_state_diff`. If Soul state changes in the same turn, that change must come from a separate accepted afterlife contract such as `ABODE_OFFERING`; do not hide unrelated Soul edits inside a Shining closure.

Legacy `shining_abode_state.json.pendingNativeFactionDiscovery` is not the normal queue, but if it is non-null in a pre-turn save it is an active state-local contract or malformed fail-closed evidence and must be closed or repaired before starting a new `discover_native_faction` request or sealing the Abode through client-owned `return_to_chaos_sea`. Use the row above and example 14A for object payloads; do not duplicate it into `pending_shining_abode_actions.json`.

If a Shining core action mutates the faction/project inputs used by the blessing-card gates draft (`invest_in_faction`, `complete_project`, `support_project`, `unsupport_project`, or `retire_project`) and the pre-turn state has `gates.hasOpenDraft = true`, preserve the canonical `gates` object and set `gates.isStale = true`. A stale draft cannot be used for `prepare_incarnation_package`; the player must regenerate it through `open_gates`.

### Deterministic `open_gates` Projection

If the request is accepted, the safest GM path is to copy the sanitized client preview `projectedStateFragment.afterFullShiningRoot.gates` into the persisted full `shining_abode_state.json` and then append the matching receipt. If the preview is unavailable, rebuild the draft mechanically:

- Radiance thresholds: `experience 0..99 => tier 0`, `100..219 => tier 1`, `220..379 => tier 2`, `380..579 => tier 3`, `580+ => tier 4`.
- Draft size by Radiance tier: `0=>4`, `1=>6`, `2=>7`, `3=>8`, `4=>10`; pick cap: `0=>1`, `1=>2`, `2=>2`, `3=>3`, `4=>4`.
- Radiance rarity ceiling: `tier 0 common`, `tier 1 uncommon`, `tier 2 rare`, `tier 3 rare`, `tier 4 radiant`; faction strength ceiling: `0..24 common`, `25..49 uncommon`, `50..74 rare`, `75..100 radiant`; a head card uses the lower of those ceilings.
- Candidate sources are Shining faction heads, supported completed Shining projects, and resident descent sources from canonical Shining resident bindings. Do not use Mortal factions, Mortal NPCs, retired/unsupported projects, or non-ascended residents.
- Candidate generation is deterministic from the current canonical Shining root plus resident root. Runtime de-duplicates by `dedupeKey`, keeps deterministic order, writes all candidates to `allCandidateBlessingCards`, takes the first `draftSize` into `availableBlessingCards`, and writes their ids to `shownBlessingCardIds`.
- Rerolls are local later mutations, not the accepted `open_gates` receipt. Accepted `open_gates` starts with `selectedBlessingCardIds=[]`; stale behavior is explicit: later faction/project mutations set `gates.isStale=true`, and a stale draft cannot be used for `prepare_incarnation_package`.

### Exact Shining Forge Mutation Table

Every forge action requires ordinary active Shining Abode, a valid `factionId`, a real Soul Relic `relicId`, enough resources, and at least one supported completed `refinement` project in that faction. Base costs are before any client-authored entitlement discount or service quote already embedded in the pending request: `reshape=10 Feathers/10 Light Sparks`, `retune_property=20/15`, `strengthen_band=30/20`, `stabilize_echo=25/15`, `uplift_rarity=45/30`. Required Radiance tiers are `reshape 0`, `retune 1`, `strengthen 2`, `stabilize 3`, `uplift 4`.

Forge ladders are canonical. Soul Relic rarity upgrades one step only: `common -> uncommon -> rare -> epic -> legendary`. Property band strengthens one step on the same ladder, or numeric bands increment by `+1`. Minimum property counts after rarity uplift are `common=1`, `uncommon=2`, `rare=3`, `epic=4`, `legendary=5`; if uplift would be below the minimum, the request must provide exact `addedProperties[]` and the receipt must echo them. Service multiplier by faction strength is `0..24 => 0.75`, `25..49 => 1.0`, `50..74 => 1.25`, `75..100 => 1.5`; `stabilize_echo` adds `floor(15 * serviceMultiplier)` to `companionManifestationQualityBonus`. Consumed relic refinement entitlements are client/runtime state: supported statuses are `pending_relic_entitlement` before use and `consumed` after use with `consumedAtTurn`/`consumedAtUtc`; `expired` is not a valid `relicRefinementEntitlements` status. Deadline-based `expired` statuses belong only to route/lore/descent style deferred effect arrays.

## Faction Materialization v1 — Shining Abode

Faction Materialization applies only when exact Shining faction authority exists. The supported authoring routes are:

- `native_discovery -> shining_core_action_request -> native_radiant`
- `player_founding -> shining_founding_request -> player_founded`
- `story -> exact story authority -> supported visibility`

The materialization identity is `shining_faction:<factionId>`. The canonical faction object carries `factionId`, supported `originType`, `hallId`, exact `creationProvenance`, `charter`, `currentAgenda`, `visibility`, explicit `storyAuthority` (object or `null`), `factionLifecycle`, `leadership.headActorType/headActorId/leadershipState`, `strategicMemory`, `chronicle`, `baseStrength`, `factionStrength`, `investCountThisAscension`, `projectArchetypesCountedThisAscension`, `projects`, `territorialInfluence`, `resourceLedger`, `tradeInventory`, `tradeInventoryReceipts`, `leadershipReceipts`, `leadershipHistory`, and `materialization`. Do not embed Mortal-style `purpose`, `principles`, `memory`, or `governance`, and do not invent direct `residentAffiliations`, `trade`, `storyState`, or `leadership.leaderActorIds` fields.

The materialization envelope still records exact capability booleans `runsProjects`, `holdsTerritorialInfluence`, `usesResourceLedger`, `hasResidentAffiliations`, `canTrade`, `hasLeadershipHistory`, and `usesStoryState`. Its disposition sections are evidence labels, not extra canonical containers: `projects` maps to `projects[]`, `territorialInfluence` to `territorialInfluence[]`, `resourceLedger` to `resourceLedger[]`, `residentAffiliations` to ascended `guardian_abode_residents.json.entries[].shiningFactionId`, `trade` to `tradeInventory` plus `tradeInventoryReceipts[]`, `leadershipHistory` to `leadershipHistory[]` plus `leadershipReceipts[]`, and `storyState` to `storyAuthority`. Each disposition is `populated` or `empty_by_design`; when empty, the actual canonical empty surface must be present (`[]` or explicit `null` as appropriate).

Every canonical Shining faction carries one complete materialization envelope. Any receipt-less canonical state is invalid and is rejected before normalization; there is no runtime promotion or compatibility fallback. An existing accepted Shining faction preserves its envelope, and repairs are bounded to the exact named section. Ordinary readers expose only exact `visibility = revealed`; missing, blank, or unsupported visibility fails closed. Hidden faction objects and every faction materialization envelope remain GM-private.

Resident evidence is an exact, case-sensitive identity link: `guardian_abode_residents.json.entries[].shiningFactionId` must equal the canonical `factionId`, including case. A case variant does not satisfy `residentAffiliations`, derived strength, tier, service multiplier, or trade authority. Faction repair packets treat `targetFiles` as an exact allowlist. A missing or ambiguous required actor profile includes both `game_state/meta/shining_abode_state.json` and `game_state/meta/afterlife_entity_profiles.json`; a resident-link defect includes the exact resident root, and unrelated afterlife roots remain forbidden.

Actor Materialization is separate. An active Guardian supplies actor/living-world authority but never creates Shining faction authority by implication.
